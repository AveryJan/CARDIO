# CARDIO301 | Clinical Research Documentation & GCP Compliance

A clinical research portfolio project demonstrating Good Clinical Practice (GCP), regulatory documentation review, Institutional Review Board (IRB) oversight, informed consent verification, and clinical data integrity for a simulated cardiovascular clinical trial.

## Project Components

- **Clinical Documentation Form:** Scenario-based participant visit documentation, correction tracking, and ALCOA+ data integrity principles.
- **Informed Consent Verification:** Review of required consent elements, identification of incomplete information, and documentation of corrective actions.
- **IRB Document Inventory:** Tracking of regulatory records, protocol versions, approval documentation, and outstanding evidence.
- **Regulatory Compliance Checklist:** Assessment of site qualification readiness, GCP expectations, and applicable FDA regulations.

## Regulatory and Quality Frameworks

ICH-GCP E6(R3) | FDA 21 CFR Parts 50, 56, and 312 | IRB Oversight | ALCOA+ | Clinical Documentation Quality

## Project Status

Simulated training exercise. The documentation identifies outstanding compliance issues and required follow-up actions; it does not establish actual site readiness or authorization to conduct a clinical trial.
